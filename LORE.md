# Larkspire: world bible

The original world for Broom Run. It's owned outright, so it can be trademarked, merchandised and extended. Every in-game string comes from `theme.js`. This file is the reasoning behind those strings and the rules for adding more.

## The pitch

Larkspire Academy of Magic stands on a sea cliff on the northern coast. Every night the castle rearranges its corridors, so the only reliable way to get around is by broom.

That one idea explains the whole game:

| Game mechanic | In the world |
|---|---|
| Endless, random corridor (Free Fly) | The castle reshuffles itself every night |
| Daily Flight, same course for everyone, new at 7am | Each morning at seven the castle settles one corridor for the whole school |
| One counted attempt a day | You only get one dawn flight that counts for your tower |
| Mystery parcels | The Lost Loft's owls deliver forgotten brooms and cloaks to students who fly well. Nobody knows how they choose (this covers the random odds) |
| Pity timer | (hidden) The owls never forget a loyal flyer |
| Streak | Turning up every morning, the Wrenfold way |
| House table (v1.1) | Tower lamps: distance flown lights your tower's lamp, and the brightest lamp each Monday wins the week |
| Secret passages | The Narrows: corridors the castle squeezes thin before it forgets them |
| Moving staircases | Run, badly, by Old Hesketh the Stairwarden |

## The four towers

Students *choose* their tower on their first night by climbing its stair. Nobody is sorted, which matches the spec ("pick a house on first launch") and keeps clear of the familiar sorting idea.

Each tower is a flying style, not a personality type, so each one maps to something the player actually does.

| Tower | Crest | Colours | Virtue | Motto | Flies like |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hartwell** | Stag | Oxblood and cream | Nerve | *Hold the line.* | Straight at the pillar, turning at the last second |
| **Mirewood** | Heron | Moss green and copper | Patience | *Still wings, sure flight.* | Threads the Narrows without a wobble |
| **Ashcombe** | Fox | Midnight navy and silver | Cunning | *Fly close, never touch.* | Lives for the near miss (the "Close!" mechanic) |
| **Wrenfold** | Wren | Ochre and plum | Heart | *Small wings, far flight.* | Longest streaks; turns up every morning |

The wren comes from the old folk tale of the wren who flew highest by riding on the eagle's back, which suits "small wings, far flight". The tale is public domain.

**Later hook:** each tower could get a small, cosmetic-only tower perk in v1.1, such as Ashcombe earning +1 coin per near miss. Keep perks tiny, matching the rule that items never beat skill.

## People

- **Headmistress Odile Ferrow** runs the school from a study she has to find again every morning.
- **Captain Bram Whitlow** is the flying master. He lost two fingers to the Long Gallery in 1994 and still flies it every dawn.
- **Old Hesketh** is the Stairwarden, the only person the moving staircases listen to, and only on Tuesdays.
- **Mother Corrie** keeps the Lost Loft and its forty-one owls.

## Places (future zones and obstacle names)

- **The Long Gallery**: pillars and low arches (the opening corridor)
- **The Candle Hall**: swinging chandeliers and floating candles
- **The Stairwell**: moving staircases
- **The Library Wing and the Roost**: flying books and owls
- **The Narrows**: secret passages
- **The Lost Loft**: where parcels come from
- v2 zones from the spec: **the Undercroft** (dungeon), **Gorse Wood** (forest), **the Cove** (lake/sea under the cliff)

## Legendary items (each has a line of lore in theme.js)

- **Midnight Broom**: only ever flown after dark, and it hums when the moon is out.
- **Cinderwing**: cut from the ash tree that survived the West Tower fire of 1711.
- **Aurora Glide**: made by a Wrenfold student who flew north until the sky turned green.
- **Starweave Cloak**: woven in the Lost Loft by somebody nobody remembers.
- **Ember Mantle**: always warm, and Captain Whitlow wants it back.
- **Moonmist Cloak**: smells faintly of sea fog off the Larkspire cliffs.

## Guardrails (keep it ours)

Use freely: wizard, witch, broom, wand, owl, castle, candles, portraits, potions, moving staircases, towers, scarves.

Don't use any of the following, because together they read as the famous series:
- The names or near-sound-alikes of its school, houses, sport, ball, currency, spells or characters.
- Lion, snake, eagle and badger as a set of four, or the scarlet/gold, green/silver, blue/bronze, yellow/black colour set. Our tower colours were picked to avoid both.
- Sorting by hat or ceremony. Our students choose.
- A caretaker with a cat, a hospital wing, a forest called "forbidden", a train from a numbered platform.
- Store listings or ads that say "Harry Potter-style" or "for Potter fans".

Before launch: search the UK trademark register and the App Store for "Broom Run", "Larkspire" and the four tower names.
